# LUNARA product requirements

LUNARA gives users a private place to log their cycle, mood, symptoms and sleep, explore wellness topics and review patterns. Product priorities are privacy, accessible interaction, transparent estimates and clear health-information boundaries.

Phase 04 delivered navigation and shared controls. Phase 05 delivered local accounts and per-account record persistence. Phase 06 added history-based estimates and descriptive wellness summaries. Phase 07 added sourced educational conversations with optional per-message history. Phase 08 adds filtered report downloads and a sourced wellness library. Production deployment safeguards follow in later phases.

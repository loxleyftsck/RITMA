# LUNARA user guide

Create an account and keep the recovery code somewhere private. It is the way to reset your password on this installation. Finish onboarding by choosing what you want to track and reviewing the storage consent.

- **Cycle** records the start and optional end of a period. You can correct earlier dates from history. The calendar distinguishes saved dates from estimates, and estimates appear when the history supports them.
- **Home** links to mood, journal, symptoms and sleep. **Insights** summarizes what you recorded. Gaps in your entries affect summaries and estimates.
- **AI Care** answers selected education questions in English or Indonesian. To include a summary of your saved records, choose that option for the current message. Use a clinician for diagnosis or treatment. Keep confidential details out of questions you do not need to send.
- **Health report** previews a date range and downloads a PDF or CSV. Journal notes are optional. Store the downloaded files privately.
- **Profile or Settings → Privacy & data** lets you withdraw or resume consent, download your account data as JSON, or delete the account. Withdrawal pauses new tracking and AI Care messages. Deletion requires your password and the confirmation word `DELETE`. Prior backups and downloaded copies follow their own retention.

If you cannot sign in, use **Reset password** with the recovery code and replace the old saved code with the new one. If the service is unavailable or you need help with a privacy request, contact the operator of your installation.
